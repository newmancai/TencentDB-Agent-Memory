# Single-pass assistant source audit

**Interpretation correction after independent review:** `same` below means the
selected proposition can remain true; it does not mean its unrestricted current
use is safe, nor that a history-preserving annotation is wrong. In particular,
enrollment followed by withdrawal can justify an applicability update without
refuting the historical enrollment event. The retained result field
`source_audit_unsupported_annotations` is misnamed and must not be used as an
action-error/false-deletion metric. Current applicability, temporal supplementation
and downstream harm need separate labels. These original judgments are retained
for auditability and were not fed back into the experiment.

This is an exact selected-assertion audit, not human gold or a global stale-memory label set. A past sign-up, completed event, or prior feeling remains true after later events; inferred current participation is a separate claim. Four ambiguous current-state/identity cases remain unknown. Full public text and annotations are in the local evidence archive. The audit was not fed to B, E, the reader or policy.
