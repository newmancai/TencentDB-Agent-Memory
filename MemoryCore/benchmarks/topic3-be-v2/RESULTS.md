## Route correction and continuation

Evidence is already retrieved in this cohort; further broad scanning or automatic
old-memory invalidation is not supported by these results. The next hypothesis is
query-specific evidence use: first draft an answer, then apply a bounded ordinary
review using the same sources. Compare that strong baseline before claiming a
special B+E benefit. Preserve historical facts and evaluate current applicability
at the clause and requested-property level. A future intention can contain an
already-true factual premise; treating an entire turn as hypothetical loses that
premise. Conversely, later aspirations must not replace a current job.

This is a hypothesis, **not implemented or evaluated**. Use a newly selected
development cohort (still disclose prior oracle exposure), freeze its protocol
before outputs, and charge both draft and review costs. Do not tune the current
16 items or call them a holdout. A successful review would still not prove
feedback learning: the eventual acceptance experiment must separately show a
bounded feedback-driven policy change and later quality/cost benefit, with off
and forced-failure fallback checks. No commercial acceptance claim.
